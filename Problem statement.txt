Back Office Expense Report Summarizer

PROBLEM STATEMENT
Back office teams process numerous expense reports with lengthy justifications and receipts. Reviewing these manually for policy compliance and summarization is time-consuming and error-prone, leading to processing delays and potential compliance risks. Existing systems provide limited automation in extracting key information or generating concise summaries for approvers. Automating expense report summarization would streamline approvals, reduce errors, and enhance operational efficiency.

Data Considerations:
Data includes textual expense reports, receipt transcriptions, and policy documents in text or PDF. Volume is moderate for demonstration. Preprocessing includes text extraction, formatting normalization, and OCR if needed. Privacy considerations require anonymization of sensitive financial data.
